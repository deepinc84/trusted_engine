> **Purpose:** Customer-facing proposal content reference for the Trusted estimator.  
> **Design reference:** Trusted Instant Estimate.  
> **Scope-detail reference:** Mega Comprehensive Work Plan.  
> **Important:** Use the Mega document for scope depth and sequencing, not for visual design or verbatim copying.

---

## 1. Non-Negotiable Customer Proposal Rules

### Customer-facing information that MAY be shown

The proposal may prominently show:

- Customer name
- Property address
- Proposal number and date
- Structures included in the quoted option
- Total measured roof area
- Roof area by structure when useful
- Predominant roof pitch
- Additional pitch ranges when they materially affect the work
- Roof plan / measurement diagram
- Front-of-property image
- Selected roofing system and product names
- Selected accessory product names
- Good / Better / Best / Custom final prices
- GST and customer total
- Optional structure or optional work pricing
- Detailed work scope
- Warranty information
- Payment terms
- Timeline / scheduling language
- Acceptance terms

### Quantities that MUST NOT be shown to customers

The proposal must **never expose estimating or purchasing quantities**, including:

- Bundle counts
- Roll counts
- Piece counts
- Pail / tube counts
- Boxes of fasteners
- Vent quantities
- Plumbing boot quantities
- Gooseneck quantities
- Starter quantities
- Hip/ridge cap quantities
- Linear footage used to calculate material purchasing
- Waste percentages
- Raw order quantity
- Rounded order quantity
- Effective quantity
- Internal calculated squares used for purchasing
- Labour quantities
- Disposal quantities
- Coverage values
- Supplier quantities

**Exception:** Total roof footage/area and roof pitch are customer-facing project measurements and should be shown prominently. Structure-specific roof area may also be shown.

### Internal financial information that MUST NOT be shown

Never expose:

- Supplier cost
- Unit cost
- Material subtotal
- Labour rate
- Labour cost
- General requirement cost
- Disposal cost
- Internal overhead
- Contingency
- Markup percentage
- Profit amount
- Workbook formulas
- Workbook row numbers
- Calculation details
- Company default pricing values

The proposal shows only the **reviewed customer selling price**.

# 3. Shared Scope — Applies to Roofing Systems When Selected

These clauses are written as **Trusted customer-facing scope language**. They are not intended to be copied from the Mega document verbatim.

## 3.1 Project Coordination & Property Protection

Trusted Roofing & Exteriors will coordinate the roofing work from material delivery through final inspection. Before removal begins, the crew will identify vulnerable areas around the home and take reasonable steps to protect landscaping, decks, exterior finishes, windows, doors, driveways and other areas affected by the roofing work.

Materials and equipment will be staged with consideration for access to the property and the work area. The site will remain an active construction area during installation and homeowners should keep children and pets clear of the work zone.

**Include when:** any roofing replacement is selected.

## 3.2 Removal of Existing Roofing

Existing roofing materials included in the selected scope will be removed to expose the underlying roof deck. Roofing debris will be collected and removed from the property as work progresses.

Existing accessory components scheduled for replacement, such as vents, plumbing flashings or damaged roofing accessories, will be removed as required for the new roofing system.

**Include when:** tear-off / replacement is selected.

## 3.3 Roof Deck Inspection

Once the existing roof covering has been removed, Trusted will visually inspect the exposed roof deck for obvious deterioration, rot, damage or areas that are not suitable for the new roofing system.

Decking replacement is not assumed to be included unless specifically identified in the proposal. If additional decking work is required after removal, Trusted will document the condition and obtain approval before proceeding with work that changes the agreed price, except where the signed agreement specifically provides a pre-authorized unit rate.

**Include when:** tear-off exposes the roof deck.

## 3.4 Drip Edge & Rake Edge

New pre-finished metal edge protection will be installed at the applicable eaves and rake edges using the metal profile and gauge selected for the project. The metal is intended to protect vulnerable roof edges, provide a clean finished transition and help direct runoff away from exposed wood components.

The exact material shown in the proposal should come from the selected estimator product.

**Include when:** drip/rake edge material rows are active.

## 3.5 Valley Protection

Where the roof contains valleys, the selected valley system will be installed to provide a durable drainage path through these high-flow areas.

Where a formed metal valley is selected, the proposal should identify the selected profile and gauge. Where project conditions require a different valley detail, the scope should reflect the actual selected system rather than using generic wording.

**Include when:** valley measurement > 0 and a valley product/system is active.

## 3.6 Ice & Water Protection

A self-adhered roofing membrane will be installed at the applicable vulnerable roof areas identified by the selected roofing system and project conditions. Typical applications may include eaves, valleys, penetrations and transitions where additional protection is required.

The selected membrane product should be named in the material-system summary without exposing roll quantities or internal coverage calculations.

**Include when:** ice & water material is active.

## 3.7 Roof Underlayment

The selected roof underlayment will be installed over applicable roof-deck areas in accordance with the roofing-system requirements and manufacturer instructions.

Where roof slope requires an enhanced or multi-layer underlayment detail, the proposal should automatically use the applicable low-slope wording instead of the standard underlayment clause.

**Include when:** underlayment is active.

## 3.8 Attic Ventilation

Existing and proposed attic ventilation will be considered as part of the roofing installation. Where the estimate includes replacement or added exhaust ventilation, Trusted will install the selected venting products as part of the new roof system.

Ventilation wording must reflect the actual selected vent system and should not promise a complete ventilation redesign unless that work is specifically included.

**Include when:** attic vent material is active.

## 3.9 Plumbing & Mechanical Roof Penetrations

Applicable plumbing boots, exhaust vents, goosenecks, B-vent components and related roof penetrations identified in the estimate will be addressed as part of the roofing work.

New components included in the selected scope will be integrated into the roofing system using compatible flashing and sealing methods. Existing components intended to remain will be identified accordingly.

**Include only for active penetration types.**

## 3.10 Step, Wall, Transition & Chimney Flashings

Existing roof-to-wall, step, transition and chimney flashing conditions will be addressed according to the selected estimate scope.

Where new flashing is included, Trusted will supply and install the selected flashing product and integrate it with the roofing system. Where existing flashing is specifically intended to remain, the proposal must say so.

**Important:** Never imply that all existing flashing is automatically replaced if the estimate does not include it.

## 3.11 Fasteners & Sealants

Roofing materials, exposed accessories and flashings will be secured using fasteners appropriate to the selected system and application. Exposed fastening locations will use appropriate weather-resistant fastening and sealing methods.

Compatible sealants will be used at applicable transitions, flashings and penetrations.

## 3.12 Final Cleanup & Quality Review

Trusted will remove project debris related to its work and complete a final cleanup of the work area. Accessible areas around the building will receive a magnetic sweep for roofing fasteners, and eavestroughs affected by the roofing work will be checked and cleaned of roofing debris.

A final installation review will be completed before the project is considered complete.

# 5. Euroshield Rubber Roofing System

Use this section when an Euroshield field product is selected.

Supported system families may include:

- Ranchlands Shake
- Rundle Slate
- Beaumont Shake
- Vermont Slate
- applicable HP / Hail Proof variants

The proposal wording must respond to the selected Euroshield profile rather than treating all Euroshield products identically.

## 5.1 Customer System Introduction

**{{EUROSHIELD_PRODUCT}} Rubber Roofing System**

This option replaces the asphalt field roofing with **{{EUROSHIELD_PRODUCT}}**, a manufactured rubber roofing system selected for its appearance, durability and impact-resistant roofing application.

Trusted will install the selected Euroshield field roofing together with the compatible starter, hip and ridge, membrane, underlayment and accessory components activated for the selected Euroshield profile.

## 5.2 Regular Euroshield Starter

The compatible Euroshield regular starter remains part of the roofing system wherever it is required by the selected Euroshield assembly.

**Important estimator rule:** Beaumont and Vermont do **not** replace the regular starter with the specialty starter system. The specialty starter components are additive.

## 5.3 Ranchlands / Rundle Starter Scope

When **Ranchlands Shake** or **Rundle Slate** is selected:

- Regular Euroshield starter is active.
- Specialty Starter Eave is inactive.
- Specialty Starter Rake is inactive.
- Specialty Starter Valley is inactive.

Customer wording:

A compatible Euroshield starter component will be installed as part of the selected Ranchlands or Rundle roof system to establish the required perimeter starting detail.

## 5.4 Beaumont / Vermont Specialty Starter Scope

When **Beaumont Shake**, **Vermont Slate**, or the applicable HP / Hail Proof version is selected, the roofing system includes the regular starter **plus** the additional locking starter components required by that profile.

Customer wording:

In addition to the regular Euroshield starter, the Beaumont/Vermont system includes profile-specific locking starter components at applicable eave, rake and valley locations. These components are integrated with the field roofing to form the perimeter and valley details required by the selected Euroshield profile.

The customer proposal may identify:

- Euroshield regular starter
- Eave (Locking) Starter
- Rake and Valley Starter

Do **not** display quantities or coverage.

## 5.5 Euroshield Field Roofing

Trusted will supply and install **{{EUROSHIELD_PRODUCT}}** in the selected colour/profile in accordance with the applicable Euroshield installation requirements.

Profile-specific accessory and starter details will be used where required by the selected Euroshield system.

# 7. Low-Slope / Omniseal Roofing Assembly

This section activates only when the estimator's low-slope rules require an Omniseal assembly.

It is independent from whether asphalt, Euroshield or another primary field roofing product is selected elsewhere on the project.

## 7.1 Customer System Introduction

**Low-Slope Roofing Assembly**

The project contains roof areas requiring a roofing assembly designed specifically for lower-slope conditions. These areas will receive the selected membrane system rather than being treated as a standard steep-slope shingle area.

The proposal should clearly identify the low-slope roof area visually or by structure/pitch, but should not disclose internal membrane quantities.

## 7.2 Omniseal Assembly

When activated, the proposal may identify the applicable components:

- Omniseal 410 Base (Mechanical), when selected
- Omniseal 420 Base (Self-Adhered), when selected
- Omniseal 430 Cap Sheet

Only show components actually active in the estimate.

Customer wording:

The low-slope area will receive the selected Omniseal base and cap-sheet assembly, installed as a coordinated membrane system at the roof areas identified as requiring low-slope protection. Perimeter, transition and penetration details will be integrated with the adjoining roofing system.

## 7.3 Low-Slope Transition Detailing

Transitions between low-slope membrane roofing and adjacent steep-slope roofing will be completed using the selected transition flashing and compatible membrane details.

**Include when:** low-slope and steep-slope systems meet within the quoted scope.

## Final Design Principle

**The estimator explains the math to Trusted.  
The proposal explains the project to the homeowner.**

A homeowner should understand:

- what roofing system they are buying,
- which major components are included,
- how Trusted intends to complete the work,
- what parts of the property are being quoted,
- what the project costs,
- and what warranty/terms apply.

They should not be able to reverse-engineer Trusted's purchasing quantities, labour assumptions, margins or supplier costs from the proposal.
