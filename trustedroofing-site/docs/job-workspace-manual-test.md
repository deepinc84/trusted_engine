# Guided Job Workspace manual test

1. Open **Jobs**, create a roofing-only job, and confirm the three-step customer, work, and setup flow.
2. Create a combined roofing and eavestrough/downspout job.
3. Create a vinyl-only job and confirm only enabled trade content appears.
4. Create a James Hardie-only job and configure separate areas.
5. Create a specification/tender job and confirm tender tabs appear only for that job.
6. Enter measurements from the **Measurements** tab and confirm the header reports the saved time.
7. Review defaults and missing selections in **Products** without leaving the job.
8. Review pricing, expand the internal breakdown, and set a strategic selling price in the trade editor.
9. Confirm below-cost pricing remains visible and requires an audited reason before sending.
10. Build the proposal from the **Proposal** tab.
11. Open **Images & Plans**, upload a cover photo and a plan, then launch annotation controls.
12. Preview the web proposal from **Review & Send**.
13. preview and download the generated PDF.
14. Send to the saved customer email, optionally adding CC and editing the message.
15. Open the secure customer link and sign the proposal.
16. Return to the job and confirm viewed/accepted state and signed-PDF access.
17. Create a revision and confirm the sent or accepted revision remains unchanged.
18. Open `/admin/estimates/<id>` and `/admin/proposals/<id>` and confirm each redirects into the correct job tab.
19. Repeat the main flow at a phone-sized viewport; verify no core form scrolls horizontally and the bottom actions remain usable.
20. Inspect the customer web proposal and PDF; confirm supplier cost, labour cost, source references, internal notes, and readiness warnings are absent.
